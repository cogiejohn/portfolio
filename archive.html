Yohan Domingo

A writer and artist for sustainable development. As an iskolar ng bayan studying Communication Research at the University of the Philippines Diliman, I look for ways to pay service to Filipinos. I started contributing to discussions over food security with critical perspectives, through Inquirer.net’s Letter to the Editor—with elements of solution-oriented journalism. I am currently expanding my advocacy for science-driven and human-centered policies through science communication. I do that by independently learning science media, complemented by my plans for future studies, towards and beyond my degree, to focus on mediated coastal and agroecology. For the love of science and the humanities!
